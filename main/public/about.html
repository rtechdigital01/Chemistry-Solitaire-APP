GAME ARCHITECTURE

About the Game

Interactive Science Puzzle

SciSolitaire turns science concepts into category-based card challenges. Instead of simply memorising isolated facts, learners identify relationships between terms, properties, processes, diagrams, examples and scientific ideas. Each challenge asks learners to sort cards into the correct scientific categories, helping strenghten recall while developing an understanding of how scientific ideas connect.

CORE GAME MECHANICS:

Supportive Learning

Mistakes lead to hints, explanations and opportunities to retry rather than harsh failure screens.

Connection-Based Matching

Match science cards with the category they belong to and learn through meaningful scientific relationships.

Immediate Feedback

Recieve clear feedback on matches, helping learners recognize errors and strengthen scientific understanding.

Progressive Challenge

Move through increasingly challenging science content as knowledge, accuracy and confidence develop.

EDUCATIONAL MISSION

About the Platform

Learner-Focused

SciSolitaire is an interactive science learning platform created to make Biology, Chemistry and Physics more approachable, engaging and easier to understand. The platform combines science education, accessibilty and game-based learning to provide learners with short, visual and rewarding learning experiences across different educational pathways.

PLATFORM COMMITMENTS:
⬡

Learning Insights for Teachers

Gameplay can generate useful progress and concept-level information for authorised teachers, helping them identify recurring difficulties and decide what learners may need to revisit.

♡

Accessible by Design

Clear layouts, concise text, visual support, optional hints and supportive feedback help create a comfortable experience for different learners.

◎

Learning across Borders

SciSolitaire is designed to support different education systems through country and learning-stage selection, rather than assuming a single national pathway.

♢

Ad-Free Learning

The learning experience is designed without distracting advertisements, helping learners focus on science, gameplay and progress.
