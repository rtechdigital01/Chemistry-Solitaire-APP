Why Learn Through Card-Matching?

SciSolitaire turns retrieval practice into short matching challenges. Learners actively recall information, recognize relationships between concepts and receive feedback as they play.

Active Recall

Matching prompts learner to retrieve information and recongnize relationships rather than simply re-read notes.

Topic by Topic

Each deck explores one key science topic in depth, helping you focus on the concepts that matter most for learning, revision, and assessments.

Progress Rewarded

Earn coins for every correct match. Track your score, completion time, and hints used — celebrating progress at every step.

Designed for Everyone

Plain English mode, adjustable text size, high contrast, and reduced motion make Science Solitaire accessible to all learners.
